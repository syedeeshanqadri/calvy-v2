## What Is Calvy?

Calvy is an online appointment booking platform for service businesses. When a business signs up, they instantly get:

1. **A personal subdomain** — `{businessname}.calvy.io` — no setup, no IT, ready immediately
2. **A professional booking page** — clients book 24/7 at `{businessname}.calvy.io/book-now`, no phone call needed
3. **A public business website** — a beautiful single-page site auto-generated and fully customizable
4. **A management dashboard** — handle appointments, clients, staff, payments, expenses, and analytics
5. **A chatbot booking widget** — a conversational AI-style booking assistant embedded on the website

The whole setup takes under 60 seconds. No tech skills required. The free plan is lifetime — no credit card, no trial countdown, no expiry.

Calvy fixes all of this in one place — and gets them online in under a minute.

## Key Features (Plain English)

### For the Business Owner

**Personal Subdomain & Booking Page**
Every business instantly gets their own web address — `{businessname}.calvy.io`. Clients book at `{businessname}.calvy.io/book-now`. No generic link, no shared platform branding. It looks like your own website.

**Business Website**
A full single-page website at `{businessname}.calvy.io` — hero section, about, services, team, and contact info — all editable from the dashboard. Upload your own photos, pick from a curated library, or paste any image URL. Everything updates live.

**Chatbot Booking Widget**
A smart conversational booking assistant floats on your website. Clients click the chat button, pick a service, choose a date and time, enter their details — all without leaving your website. Works like a real booking assistant, 24/7. *(Available on Professional and Business plans.)*

**Dashboard**
A clean, easy-to-use control panel with:
- Today's appointments at a glance
- Revenue stats and charts (only counting paid appointments)
- Client directory with full visit history and ledger
- Staff management with working hours
- Service management (name, price, duration in your currency)

**Expense Tracking & Profit**
Log business expenses (rent, salaries, supplies, etc.) and instantly see monthly profit = revenue − expenses. No spreadsheets needed. *(Professional and Business plans.)*

**Client Ledger**
Each client has a detail page showing their full appointment history, total paid, last visit date — a clear ledger of every interaction. Automatic VIP badge for top spenders.

**Online Payments**
Accept payment upfront when a client books (via Stripe). Revenue accumulates in a wallet. Request payout to bank account whenever ready. Or use postpaid mode — client pays at the appointment, mark it paid in the dashboard. *(Prepaid/Stripe requires Professional plan or above.)*

**Broadcasts**
Send bulk email or WhatsApp messages to your entire client list in one click — promotions, reminders, updates, or seasonal offers. *(Professional and Business plans.)*

**Automatic Notifications**
Clients receive a confirmation email (with a calendar invite attached) and a WhatsApp message the moment they book. A reminder is sent before their appointment. The business owner gets a browser push notification for every new booking.

**Analytics**
See which services are most popular, which clients spend the most, busiest days of the week, 12-month revenue and profit trends. *(Professional and Business plans.)*

**Multi-Currency**
Works in 15+ currencies. Each business picks their currency at signup — prices are stored and displayed in that currency, no conversion math.

**Staff Management**
Add team members, assign them to services, set their working hours, and color-code them on the calendar.

**Group Booking**
Set a capacity on any service to allow multiple clients to book the same time slot — perfect for classes, workshops, or group sessions. *(Professional and Business plans.)*

**Calendar View**
See all appointments in a monthly calendar. Know at a glance how busy each day is.

**Website Builder**
Customize the public website from the dashboard — change text, images, colors, toggle sections on/off. SEO controls (page title, meta description) available on Professional and Business plans.

**CSV Export**
Export appointments and payments as a spreadsheet for accounting or records. *(Starter and above.)*

**Priority Support**
Direct support ticket access with fast responses from the Calvy team. *(Business plan.)*

### For the Client (End User / Person Booking)

- Visits `{businessname}.calvy.io/book-now` or chats with the booking widget
- Picks a service (or multiple), chooses a staff member (optional), selects a date and time
- Enters name, email, phone number
- Pays online or confirms (depending on business settings)
- Gets a confirmation email with a calendar invite instantly
- Gets a WhatsApp message confirmation
- Gets a reminder before the appointment
- No account, no app, no friction

## Objection Handling

**"It's too expensive."**
The Professional plan is $2/month billed annually — less than a cup of coffee. The Starter plan is $1/month annually. And the free plan covers getting started with no time limit and no credit card.

**"I'm not good with technology."**
No setup needed. Sign up, verify your email, and your subdomain, booking page, and website are ready instantly. No coding, no design, no IT help required.

**"I already have a website."**
Calvy's booking page works independently. Share the `{yourname}.calvy.io/book-now` link on Instagram, WhatsApp, or your existing website. Or embed the chatbot widget. You don't need to rebuild anything.

**"My clients prefer to call."**
That's fine — they still can. But many clients (especially younger ones) will choose to book online if given the option. You'll get more bookings, not fewer.

**"I'm worried my clients won't use it."**
The booking flow is 5 simple steps and takes under 2 minutes. No account required. If they can shop online, they can book with Calvy.

**"I work in a different currency."**
Calvy supports 15+ currencies. You pick your currency when you sign up — everything is shown in your local currency, no conversions.

**"I don't need a chatbot."**
The chatbot is optional — it only shows on your website if your plan includes it. Every plan includes the standard booking page at your own subdomain.
