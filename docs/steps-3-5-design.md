## Schema (one migration per chunk, `prisma migrate dev`)
- **Enums:** `PackId {CLINIC, SALON_SPA, LAWYER, FITNESS, OTHER}`, `BookingMode {SLOT, TOKEN}`, `OnlinePayMode {BOOKING_FEE_ONLY, FULL_ONLINE}`, `FeePayer {CUSTOMER, BUSINESS}`, `QueueState {WAITING, SERVING, DONE, NO_SHOW, MOVED}`, `BookingSource {ONLINE, DASHBOARD, WALK_IN}`, `PayProvider {MOCK, CASHFREE}` (STRIPE added in Step 7), `PayStatus {CREATED, PAID, FAILED, EXPIRED, REFUNDED}`, `FeeEntryKind {CHARGED, CARRIED_OVER, WAIVED, REFUNDED}`.
- **Business:** `packId PackId @default(OTHER)` (backfill from `businessType`), `country String` (ISO-2, **no default**, required at signup, pre-filled from the detected region; backfill `currency = INR` → `IN`, else `US`), `bookingMode @default(SLOT)`, `onlinePayMode @default(BOOKING_FEE_ONLY)`, `feePayer @default(CUSTOMER)`, `termsOverride Json?`. Keep `primaryColor` as the theme colour.
- **TokenSession** (new): `id, businessId, staffId String?` (null = business-wide queue), `name, weekdays Int[], startMin, endMin, minutesPerToken, isActive`. Capacity = floor((end-start)/minutes). Numbering, capacity and ETA are per session row, so per session + staff.
- **Appointment:** `source @default(ONLINE)`, `holdExpiresAt DateTime?` (status `pending_payment`), `tokenSessionId?`, `sessionDate Date?`, `tokenNumber Int?`, `queuePosition Int?`, `queueState?`, `estimatedAt Timestamptz?`, `notPresentCount Int @default(0)`, `carriedFromId String? @unique` (at most one carry-over). `@@unique([tokenSessionId, sessionDate, tokenNumber])`, `@@index([businessId, sessionDate, queuePosition])`.
- **Payment** (new): `businessId, appointmentId, provider, providerOrderId @unique, amountMinor, platformFeeMinor, businessShareMinor, currency, status, method?, rawRef`. **PlatformFeeEntry** (ledger, append-only): `businessId, appointmentId, paymentId?, kind, amountMinor, createdAt`. **PayoutAccount**: `businessId @unique, provider, accountId, kycStatus`. **WebhookEvent**: `provider, eventId` unique (idempotency).
- **PlanConfig:** `platformFeeMinorIN Int @default(0)` (Free = 1000; paid = 0). International never charged.

## Booking flow per mode
- **Slot (today):** unchanged unless payment needed. **Token:** customer picks date + session, sees "~N left"; on confirm, in one transaction with advisory lock on (session, date): count tokens incl. live holds, `n = max+1`, `estimatedAt = start + (n-1)*min`, reject if `n > capacity`.
- **Who pays online:** India Free: every PUBLIC online booking (website, booking page, widget) pays Rs 10, no opt-out (upgrade removes it); dashboard bookings and walk-ins are free. International at launch: pay at venue only, no online payment.
- **Payment needed?** `fee = country=IN ? plan.platformFeeMinorIN : 0`. BOOKING_FEE_ONLY charges `fee` (Calvy keeps it; no business KYC). FULL_ONLINE: payer CUSTOMER charges `P+fee`, business gets `P`; payer BUSINESS charges `P`, business gets `P-fee`. Charge 0 → confirm immediately. Gateway fees absorbed by Calvy.
- **Pending + hold:** create Appointment `pending_payment` + slot/token hold (`holdExpiresAt = now+10m`) + Payment `CREATED`; checkout lists UPI first ("Recommended"). Webhook `PAID` (idempotent) → confirm, write fee ledger, send email. `FAILED`/timeout → release (lazy on read + cron sweep); token numbers of released holds are not reused. Paid after expiry → auto-refund. Group-service holds count against capacity until expiry.
- **Refunds:** business cancels a paid booking → full refund incl. Rs 10 (ledger `REFUNDED`). Customer cancels → Rs 10 kept; in FULL_ONLINE the service fee is refunded only before the cancellation cutoff (Step 2 rule).
- **Receipt:** Rs 10 is GST-inclusive; a simple receipt (Calvy / Raybit legal name, address, GSTIN from config, never hard-coded) is attached to the confirmation email and viewable on the manage page.
- **Queue actions:** Next (SERVING → DONE, next WAITING → SERVING). Not present #1: position += 2 (or end), recompute ETAs, email new position/time. #2: NO_SHOW, closed, email, no refund. Not coming: MOVED; new appointment same session next working day at end of queue, `carriedFromId`, ledger `CARRIED_OVER`, no new charge, email. Max one carry-over: a second Not coming closes the appointment (email, no refund). Walk-in: source WALK_IN, fee 0, token at end.
- **Live tracking:** `/{slug}/q/{signedCode}` polls 15s: "Now serving #8 · you are #12 · ~25 min"; no PII.

## Pack extras (added 2026-10-24, used in chunk 3)
Each `Pack` also carries `placeholders` (service name + description) and `staffRoles` (dropdown options). Every label and placeholder on the services, staff, clients and appointment forms comes from `t()` and the pack, none hard-coded.
- **Service form placeholders** (name / description): Clinic "e.g. General Consultation" / "e.g. First visit with the doctor, includes checkup"; Salon & Spa "e.g. Haircut & Style" / "e.g. Wash, cut and blow-dry"; Lawyer "e.g. Initial Legal Consultation" / "e.g. 30-minute discussion of your case"; Fitness "e.g. Personal Training Session" / "e.g. One-on-one workout with a trainer"; Other "e.g. Standard Appointment" / "e.g. What the customer gets".
- **Staff role dropdown** (only roles clients book with, no Receptionist/Nurse/Paralegal; plus "Other" with free text): Clinic: Doctor, Dentist, Physiotherapist, Psychologist. Salon & Spa: Stylist, Barber, Beautician, Therapist, Nail Technician. Lawyer: Advocate, Senior Advocate, Associate. Fitness: Trainer, Yoga Instructor, Coach, Nutritionist. Other: Consultant, Specialist, Instructor. `Staff.role` stays a free string; a stored value not in the list shows as "Other: <value>", so existing data stays valid. Dashboard labels say "Staff"; public pages label the person by their shared role.

## Email templates (all via `t()`)
Slot confirmed (+ fee receipt), Token confirmed (number, ETA, tracking link), Payment failed / hold expired, Moved back (new position + ETA), No-show closed, Moved to next day, Owner: new paid booking. Walk-in only if email given.

1. `step-3/packs-terms-engine`: schema PackId/termsOverride, `lib/packs`, `lib/terms`, backfill. Tests: term fallback, plural, override, pack Zod validation.
2. `step-3/packs-onboarding`: six packs' defaults (Individual Consultant added 2026-10-24: solo experts, terms client / consultant / consultation / session), pack picker at signup + settings, idempotent apply. Tests: apply twice harmless, defaults valid.
3. `step-3/terms-rollout`: `t()` across website, booking page, dashboard, emails. Tests: snapshot of rendered email strings per pack.
4. `step-3/theme-colour`: presets + picker on all plans, CSS var on website + booking page. Tests: hex/contrast helper.
4b. `step-3/website-extras` (after chunk 4): **Logo** upload (PNG/JPG/SVG/WebP, max 2 MB, auto-resized) shown in the website header, booking page header, confirmation/reminder emails and as favicon; falls back to initials in the theme colour. Type checked by file content, not extension; SVG is never served raw (rasterised or sanitised server-side, keeping the S1-22 no-raw-SVG rule). **Storage adapter:** local disk in dev, S3-compatible (Cloudflare R2) in production, so uploads do not need Vercel. **Social links:** Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp (number to `wa.me`), X, LinkedIn, YouTube, website, as icons in the website footer and booking page. **"Leave a Google review"** button from a pasted review link (help text: how to copy it from Google Business Profile), on the website, the booking success page and later the post-visit email (Step 6). **Google Maps** embed from the address in the contact section and an **opening-hours block** generated from business hours. Website editor gets a "Brand & links" section with live preview. Tests: upload validation, social URL validation and normalisation, review-link validation.
5. `step-4/token-sessions`: TokenSession, bookingMode, settings + capacity preview. Tests: capacity, overlapping sessions rejected.
6. `step-4/token-booking`: atomic assignment, token booking UI, confirmation email. Tests: numbering, ETA, capacity full (pure part).
7. `step-4/queue-screen`: walk-ins, Next / Not present / Not coming, ETA recompute, emails. Tests: move-back-2, fewer than 2 behind → moves to end, second skip = no-show, next-working-day, second Not coming closes, ETA after skips, per-staff numbering.
8. `step-4/live-tracking`: public page + signed code. Tests: code sign/verify, payload has no PII.
9. `step-5/payments-core`: enums, Payment, ledger, WebhookEvent, provider interface + mock, `lib/fees`. Tests: fee matrix (IN free/paid, intl, both payers, both modes), mock contract, idempotency.
10. `step-5/pending-holds-checkout`: pending booking, hold expiry + sweep, UPI-first mock checkout, refund-after-expiry. Tests: expiry/release logic, token not reused, group hold counts against capacity, refund rules (business vs customer cancel, cutoff).
11. `step-5/online-payment-settings`: country (signup + backfill), mode, payer, plan waiver, PayoutAccount screen, GST receipt. Tests: settings Zod, waiver resolution, backfill mapping, receipt totals.
12. `step-5/cashfree-split`: Cashfree adapter (test creds), vendor onboarding, split at order. Tests: signature verify, split amounts.
13. `step-5/subscriptions-cashfree`: Cashfree subscriptions for India Pro/Business (monthly/yearly), plan activation via webhook, downgrade to Free on expiry; then remove Razorpay code and retire wallet/withdrawals. Tests: subscription state machine, webhook idempotency, expiry downgrade.
