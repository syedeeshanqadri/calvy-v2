## Internal link rules
- Every spoke links **up** to its pillar (keyword anchor) and the pillar links **down** to every spoke.
- 2–3 sibling links within a cluster; 0–1 cross-cluster (e.g. Pillar 1 "deposits via UPI" ↔ Pillar 2 "deposit policy template").
- Help-center articles (Razorpay setup, payments) link into Pillar 1 spokes; Pillar 1 spokes link back to help docs.
- No orphan pages; every post reachable from a pillar in 2 clicks.
